# Work From Home Policy

## Eligibility and Approval

Employees may work from home (WFH) with prior approval from their reporting manager.

Agree the WFH day with your manager before you start working from home.

## Recording Work From Home Days

On an approved work from home day, clock in on the Attendance page with the WFH work mode.

WFH days appear on the Team Calendar so that your team can see who is working remotely.

## Working Securely From Home

Sensitive company data must be accessed only on approved devices.

Company laptops must not be shared with unauthorized users, including family members.

Report any suspected security incident to IT within 1 hour.
