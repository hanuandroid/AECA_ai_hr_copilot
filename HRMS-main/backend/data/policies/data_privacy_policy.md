# Employee Data Privacy Policy

## Access to Employee Data

Employee personal data must be accessed only for approved HR operations.

Sharing employee records outside authorized systems is prohibited.

## Devices and Classification

Sensitive company data must be accessed only on approved devices.

Documents must be tagged based on internal classification guidelines.

## Breaches and Monitoring

Any suspected data breach must be reported to IT Security within 1 hour.

Access logs are reviewed monthly by the compliance team.
