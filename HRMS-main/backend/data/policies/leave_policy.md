# Leave Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all NovaWorks employees who use CB Nest to request leave.

## Leave Types and Annual Entitlement

NovaWorks provides three types of leave each year:

- Casual leave: 12 days per year.
- Sick leave: 10 days per year.
- Earned leave: 15 days per year.

Your current balance for each leave type (total, used and remaining days) is shown on the Leaves page in CB Nest.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per year.

Emergency sick leave must be informed to your reporting manager before 10 AM on the day of absence. Record the absence in CB Nest as a leave request with the leave type Sick.

## Casual and Earned Leave

Casual leave (12 days per year) is intended for personal matters and short absences.

Earned leave (15 days per year) is intended for planned time off such as vacations.

## Applying for Leave

Planned leave should be requested at least 2 days in advance.

Every leave request must include the leave type, a start date, an end date and a reason. The end date must be on or after the start date.

You cannot submit a leave request whose dates overlap with one of your existing pending or approved leave requests.

## Half-Day Leave

Half-day leave is allowed. A half-day leave request must have the same start date and end date, and you must choose either the first half or the second half of the day.

A half-day leave counts as 0.5 day against your leave balance.

## Approval Process

New leave requests start with the status Pending.

Your reporting manager or an HR administrator reviews the request and either approves or rejects it. Only pending requests can be approved or rejected.

Leave days are deducted from your balance when the request is approved. A request cannot be approved if there is not enough remaining balance for that leave type.

Leave requests submitted by HR administrators for themselves are approved automatically.
